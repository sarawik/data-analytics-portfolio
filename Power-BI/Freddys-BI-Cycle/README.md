# Freddy's BI-cycle – Business Intelligence Analysis

## Project Overview

An individual Business Intelligence project with four interactive dashboards covering different business areas: sales, human resources, inventory management and financial performance.

The project focused on exploring business data, developing relevant performance indicators and creating visualizations to support analysis across the different business functions.

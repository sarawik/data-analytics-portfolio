# Freddy's BI-cycle – Business Intelligence Analysis

## Project Overview

An individual Business Intelligence project involving the development of four interactive dashboards covering different business areas: sales, human resources, inventory management and financial performance.

The project focused on exploring business data, developing relevant performance indicators and creating visualizations to support analysis across different functions of the business.

## Dashboards

### 1. Sales Analysis
Explores sales performance through relevant metrics and visualizations, providing insights into business sales data.

### 2. HR Analysis
Examines human resources data through workforce-related metrics and performance indicators.

### 3. Inventory Analysis
Analyzes inventory data to provide insights into stock levels and inventory-related performance.

### 4. Financial Analysis
Explores financial data through relevant calculations and key performance indicators to support business performance monitoring.

## Tools & Techniques

- Power BI Desktop
- DAX measures and calculations
- KPI development
- Data visualization
- Business performance analysis

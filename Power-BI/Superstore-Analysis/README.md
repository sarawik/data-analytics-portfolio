# Superstore – Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

A collaborative Business Intelligence project based on the Superstore dataset from [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets), focusing on sales performance and profitability. The objective was to develop interactive dashboards that provide insights at different organizational decision-making levels, from strategic management to day-to-day operations.

The project involved designing four dashboards, each tailored to different analytical needs and time horizons.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze sales performance and profitability across products, regions and cities.
- Identify trends, patterns and areas of underperformance.
- Develop dashboards adapted to different organizational decision-making levels.
- Support data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations.

## Key Findings

- Identified the Central region as the least profitable region.
- Found that Texas had consistently weak profitability within the Central region.
- Analyzed the most and least profitable cities in Texas to identify geographical differences in performance.
- Investigated Austin at the operational level, identifying individual products with negative profit margins. Low selling prices and discounts were potential contributing factors.

The analysis demonstrated how profitability issues identified at a regional level could be investigated further through more detailed operational data.
